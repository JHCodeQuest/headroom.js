More room for your content.

Scroll down to tuck the header away. Scroll up to bring it back. This fork uses element events for state notifications.

Header tracking enabled

Element events. A modern setup.

This example imports the ES module and subscribes to header events with TypeScript. The button tests cleanup and re-initialisation. Page cleanup keeps cached pages working and removes subscriptions when the page is discarded.

Designed to stay out of the way.

The header responds to scroll direction. A tolerance of five pixels filters small movements, and a sixty-pixel offset keeps it visible near the top.

Small details matter.

Keyboard focus brings the header into view. The reduced-motion preference removes the slide transition.
